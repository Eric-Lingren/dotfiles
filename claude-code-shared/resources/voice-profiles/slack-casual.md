## Intent

This profile targets informal Slack replies where the audience is internal (engineering
or cross-functional team members). The expected register is: direct, low-hedging,
contraction-heavy, one-to-three sentences.

## Layer 1 — paste-ready rules (placeholder)

1. Keep replies short. One to three sentences.
2. Use contractions: don't, can't, couldn't, isn't.
3. State findings directly. Skip hedging words ("perhaps", "it seems", "might be").
4. No greeting or sign-off. Get straight to the point.
5. Use plain language. Avoid jargon unless the audience is engineers.
